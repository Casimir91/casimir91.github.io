Quick quiz

Answer by instinct. No air resistance.

A feather and a piano fall from \(100\ \text{m}\). Which lands first?

  • the piano
  • the piano, by a little
  • ✓ same time

No air: landing speed depends on height, not on mass.

A car at \(50\ \text{km/h}\) stops in \(14\ \text{m}\). At \(100\ \text{km/h}\), how many metres to stop?

  • \(14\ \text{m}\)
  • \(28\ \text{m}\)
  • ✓ \(56\ \text{m}\)

Energy × 4, so distance × 4.

Two balls leave a window at the same speed: one down, one up. Which lands faster?

  • the one thrown down
  • the one thrown up
  • ✓ same speed

Same energy at the start, same energy at the end.

Box of \(3\ \text{kg}\), shelf from \(0.5\ \text{m}\) to \(1.5\ \text{m}\). Gain in potential energy:

  • \(15\ \text{J}\)
  • ✓ \(30\ \text{J}\)
  • \(45\ \text{J}\)
  • \(450\ \text{J}\)

\(m g \Delta h = 3 \times 10 \times 1 = 30\ \text{J}\)

Book of \(2\ \text{kg}\) lifted \(1.2\ \text{m}\): straight up, or along a ramp. Gain in potential energy:

  • bigger on the ramp
  • smaller on the ramp
  • ✓ the same: \(24\ \text{J}\)
  • \(0\ \text{J}\)

Only \(\Delta h\) counts, not the path.

Push \(120\ \text{N}\) over \(5\ \text{m}\). Work:

  • \(24\ \text{J}\)
  • \(125\ \text{J}\)
  • ✓ \(600\ \text{J}\)
  • \(4800\ \text{J}\)

\(W = F d = 600\ \text{J}\)
