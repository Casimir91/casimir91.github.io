Creative Tools

Groovebox

A small rack of electronic instruments: launch ready-made loops (drums, bass, guitar, keys) that change at the next bar, play 16 MPC-style pads and a keyboard, and bend the whole mix with a Kaoss-style XY pad (filter, reverb, echo, flanger). Shape each sound and watch it on the oscilloscope.

Loops · song timeline · pads · keys · XY effects · waveshaper · granular · export WAV/MP3 · works on smartphone
