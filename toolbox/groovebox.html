Chords: the 4-bar progression

The loop is 4 bars. Each bar plays one degree of the scale (I, II, III…): bass, guitar and keys move along the scale, so the chord quality (major, minor, diminished…) comes from the scale itself. Change the scale and the whole progression changes colour.

A block is a 4-bar loop; every column of the ruler is one cycle (the bar numbers are the first bar of each). Pick a loop below, then tap or drag in its lane to place it. Scenes drop six loops at the pink cursor (click the ruler to move it). While the song plays you can still edit: changes enter at the next cycle. Key, scale, chords and tempo stay as you set them in Loops.

Note Repeat

Bank A has chords in several colours (organ, Rhodes, strings, stabs; V is the keyboard sound), guitar and bass. Bank B has sung phrases (soul, gospel, R&B style). They are synthesised by the browser with formant synthesis, not recordings of real singers; the pitch follows the key and the chord of the bar, and the Voice note chips choose which note of the scale. With Note Repeat on, hold a pad: it re-triggers in time with the beat (like the MPC). Rec records your hits over the 4 bars and loops them. On a computer the pads are the keys 1 2 3 4 / Q W E R / A S D F / Z X C V.

Mixer

Waveshaper off

A transfer function y = f(x): every sample of the master goes in as x and comes out as f(x). A straight line leaves the sound alone; a curve that flattens (soft clip) adds harmonics. Draw your own curve on the plot, pick a preset, or build it from the Lab harmonics (Chebyshev: Tₙ(cos θ) = cos nθ). The shaded band is where your music sits on the curve: more drive means a wider band. It also acts on the Drums / Bass stems when you record.

Curve
Test

Chebyshev sliders. f(x) = Σ aₙ·Tₙ(x), with Tₙ(cos θ) = cos nθ. Start the test tone (a quiet sine of amplitude exactly 1, sent through the same curve, independent of Drive and Mix) and raise H3: a sine through T₃ comes out as its 3rd harmonic only. The dotted lines on the spectrum mark k·f₀.

Granular cloud no sample

The sample is cut into tiny grains (3–500 ms), each with a smooth window, scattered around a position and played hundreds per second: a cloud. Short grains of Δt ms smear the pitch by about Δf ≈ 1/Δt. Grab what the groove is playing, use a test sound (sine sweep: position = pitch; plucked chords), record 4 s from the microphone, or load a file of yours: everything is decoded inside your browser, never uploaded and never saved (it disappears when you reload). Use only audio you have the right to use. The cloud goes into the mix before the master filter and effects, so it is in the master recording but not in the Drums / Bass stems.

Export · record

Records what you hear and saves it. Press Rec: it starts on the next bar line (or with Play), and Stop & save ends it. Or pick a number of bars and it stops by itself, perfect for loops. The ZIP holds the Master (the whole mix) plus Drums and Bass on their own, each with its fader, mute, filter, reverb and XY effects as you played them. The final compressor acts only on the Master.

    Ghost = a soft hit (funk snare). Roll = the step is split in two or three quick hits (trap hi-hat). Tap a filled step with the same brush to clear it.

    Bass line

    Each row is a note of the scale. Tap to place a note of the chosen length, or drag to the right to hold it longer: the note keeps sounding (sustain) until its end. A new note cuts the previous one.

    Selected: Kick
    Guitar

    Chord stab and Pluck follow the bass: they play the note under the step (the key note when the bass is silent). Use the brush of the Drums section for ghost notes and rolls.

    Selected: Kick
    Keys · chords

    Chords: each active step plays the whole chord. The notes are stacked thirds taken from the scale you chose (Key and Scale), on the bass note of the step. Chords, Low and Arp play at the same time.

    Selected: Kick
    Keys · low & static

    Low: a quiet line underneath: Root, Fifths, Octaves, or a slow walk (Up / Scale). It fills the bottom without taking the lead.

    Selected: Kick
    Keys · arpeggio

    Arp plays the notes of the chord one at a time: each active step takes the next note. Up/Down/Random use the chord; Scale ↑ ↓ ↕ climb the notes of the chosen scale one by one. The pitches always follow the Scale, the Key and the chord progression.

    Selected: Kick
    Sound lab

    Move a slider: the white trace is the sound before, the green one is the sound now.

    Custom waveform · harmonics 8 harmonics

    Every steady sound is a sum of sine waves: the fundamental (ω) and its harmonics (2ω, 3ω…). Slide the harmonics to build your own timbre, or start from an instrument. Then pick Custom as the Waveform of any sound (Sound lab, Arp, Chords, Low, Bass) or in the Keys sound list.

    Oscilloscope · the whole mix last hit
    Triggered on the last hit: all the sounds that play together, added.
