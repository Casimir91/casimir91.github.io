Arkana

Associative cards for building an argument.

How to use it at home

  1. Choose a topic.
  2. Draw the cards.
  3. Think for thirty seconds.
  4. Speak out loud and let yourself go.

Arkana is meant to be used on your own: a help to build an argument, open your mind and unlock your reasoning.
