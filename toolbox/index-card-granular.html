Waveshaper & Granular

Two ways to sculpt a sound. Draw the transfer curve y = f(x) and watch the harmonics appear in the spectrum, or build them from Chebyshev polynomials. Then cut a sound (sweep, chords, microphone or your own file) into grains and play with position, spray, size, density and pitch.
